adaptations for this project's NR pipeline.

Reference files:
  OptiScaler/dlssnr/DlssNrFeature_Dx12.cpp

Adaptation in OptiGazeScaler:
  OptiScaler/upscalers/dlssnr/DLSSNRFeature_Dx12.cpp
  NGX exposure acquisition and the EffectivePaperWhite shader helper

This is a modified implementation. It reads the live NGX exposure texture on
the GPU and uses PreExposure * ExposureScale / ExposureTexture, with invalid
input checks and a manual-white fallback. These details describe the current
adaptation of the referenced exposure-texture approach. The pinned checkout
and earlier white-point commit record consulted source snapshots and related
work, not the exact revision of every approach consulted during development
or a line-by-line import revision. The current HUDfix display-image
route and ROI edge-extrapolation algorithm are separate from this adaptation.
