Current status (2026-09-20)
---------------------------

The optional ratio-composition path described below was removed from the
production implementation. This notice and its full MIT license are retained
for attribution continuity. The current scene exposure path is documented
separately in OptiScaler_DLSSNR_ATTRIBUTION.txt.

Historical derivation
-----------------------

The optional ratio composition in
OptiScaler/upscalers/dlssnr/DLSSNRFeature_Dx12.cpp is taken from RenoDX's
DLSS 5 Neural Rendering addon. Specifically:

  * UpgradeToneMap -- the two-branch luminance ratio: below the proxy's
    luminance the original's own luminance is the target, and above it the
    difference is treated as headroom the proxy could not represent and handed
    back on top of the model's answer.

  * The OkLab hue correction applied to the rescaled result, which takes the
    hue and chroma direction from the model's own output and only the chroma
    magnitude from the scaled colour.

  * The shadow-floor regularisation and two-sided luminance-ratio guard.

  * The reversible neutral-axis gamut compression: the D65 adaptation state
    carried in a Hunt-Pointer-Estevez LMS basis, the largest scale toward the
    neutral colour of equal luminance that leaves no channel negative, and the
    compression itself.

This is their design. It was reimplemented here in the scene-linear domain
with different names and comments, which does not make it original work, and
it is credited as theirs.

What is not derived from it
---------------------------

The OkLab conversion matrices are Bjorn Ottosson's published constants. The
linear-light transfer adaptation is standard colour science and is not specific
to RenoDX.
